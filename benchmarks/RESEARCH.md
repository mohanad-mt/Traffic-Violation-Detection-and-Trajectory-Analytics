# Research and data notes

The initial bottleneck was missing vehicle observations on difficult cameras. Better association can preserve a trajectory across a short gap. A vehicle that remains absent or below the track-start cutoff needs a detector or data change. The experiment compares these effects independently before comparing the alert output.

## What to reuse

| Approach | Evidence and implementation | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| YOLOX | Apache-2.0 upstream code and COCO weights. Its decoupled head provides objectness and class scores, and the official ONNX files run with a small OpenCV/ORT adapter. Zoo S takes RGB, upstream M takes BGR. [Source](https://github.com/Megvii-BaseDetection/YOLOX) | Keep S as the reference and M as a measured alternative. M was strongest on the external fog images. |
| RT-DETR | Apache-2.0 code and the pinned R18 checkpoint. Set prediction ranks boxes without standard NMS. The authors report 46.5 COCO AP for R18 at 640 px. The application uses the official preprocessing and PyTorch CUDA or CPU. [Implementation](https://github.com/lyuwenyu/RT-DETR) · [Checkpoint](https://huggingface.co/PekingU/rtdetr_r18vd) | Retain it as an option. The development stop result improved, while nighttime wrong-way recall stayed zero. |
| RF-DETR and newer YOLO models | RF-DETR-N has Apache-2.0 weights and reports 48.4 COCO AP with 2.3 ms T4 TensorRT FP16 latency. Other weights can have different terms. Ultralytics YOLO11 and YOLO26 code/weights have AGPL or commercial terms. The reported TensorRT timings cannot be compared directly to our end-to-end measurements. [RF-DETR models](https://github.com/roboflow/rf-detr) · [Ultralytics terms](https://www.ultralytics.com/license) | RF-DETR is a reasonable candidate for a subsequent fixed-camera test. The upstream implementations and model packages were reviewed. |
| SAHI | Apache-2.0 toolkit for slicing, inference and box merging. Published gains are on small-object benchmarks such as VisDrone and UAVDT. More crops increase model calls. [Implementation and paper](https://github.com/obss/sahi) | Keep the existing crop implementation as the experimentally controlled variant. DAWN results provide little reason to add another tiled inference dependency now. |
| ByteTrack | High/low score matching with a short lost-track buffer. Its original MOT17 result was 63.1 HOTA and 77.3 IDF1 at about 30 FPS on a V100. Our adapter uses the Apache-2.0 Roboflow implementation and keeps detector boxes, scores and classes. [Paper and reference code](https://github.com/FoundationVision/ByteTrack) · [Library](https://github.com/roboflow/trackers) | Retain for fewer ID switches and higher IDF1 on the independent tracking benchmark. |
| OC-SORT | Repairs motion state on reacquisition and considers observed direction. The authors benchmark HOTA and association accuracy on MOT and DanceTrack. The same Roboflow library supplies it without a ReID model. [Reference](https://github.com/noahcao/OC_SORT) | Retain as an option for short, noisy trajectories. Review its lower coverage before relying on it for distant vehicles. |
| BoT-SORT and UCMCTrack | BoT-SORT adds camera-motion and optional appearance matching. The reported MOT17 result is 65.0 HOTA and 80.2 IDF1. UCMCTrack models uncertainty after projecting observations onto the ground plane, with 62.8 HOTA on the authors' MOT20 run. Both publish code. [BoT-SORT](https://github.com/NirAharon/BoT-SORT) · [UCMCTrack](https://github.com/corfyi/UCMCTrack) | Camera compensation makes sense on clips with visible drift. Tiny clipped vehicles provide a weak basis for appearance embeddings. Ground-plane tracking needs a calibrated test camera, especially when boxes are near the horizon. |
| TrackEval | HOTA separates detection and association behavior. The implementation also supplies CLEAR and identity metrics. [Source](https://github.com/JonathonLuiten/TrackEval) | Use it directly on GRAM-RTM XML identities. |
| AI City anomaly systems | The challenge winner combines stabilization, background evidence, dynamic tracking and postprocessing. Doshi and Yilmaz emphasize an efficient roadside anomaly pipeline. The submitted solutions are useful implementation references and have separate license terms. They focus largely on abnormal stops and crashes. [Zhao et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.03827) · [Associated implementation](https://github.com/Mandroide/gpasb-nvidia2021-track4) · [Doshi and Yilmaz](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2021W/AICity/html/Doshi_An_Efficient_Approach_for_Anomaly_Detection_in_Traffic_Videos_CVPRW_2021_paper.html) · [Challenge code index](https://github.com/NVIDIAAICITYCHALLENGE/2021AICITY_Code_From_Top_Teams) | Long-window background evidence is worth testing on sustained stops. This run keeps explicit exclusion regions for expected queues and measures duplicate and onset errors. |
| Wrong-direction reference | The public wrong-direction driver implementation gives a point of comparison for direction inference from tracks. Repository availability alone does not establish usable data rights or a transferable accuracy measurement. [Source](https://github.com/Usmankhujaev/Wrong-direction-drivers-detection) | Keep lane-specific geometry, heading and minimum reverse duration. Test a time-normalized threshold on deterministic trajectories at 5 and 25 FPS first. |

### Geometry, weather and confidence

- The ground-plane path fits a homography from measured points and optionally scores separate check points. The app exports positions and timestamp-normalized velocities in the configured units. It retains pixel-space rules and makes no speed claim for the public road clips. BrnoCompSpeed has independently measured ground truth and is appropriate for a calibration study. [Project and code](https://github.com/JakubSochor/BrnoCompSpeed)
- Fine-tuning on simulated fog improved real-fog results in the Foggy Cityscapes work. Use pretrained initialization, mixed clear and adverse-weather coverage, and camera- or sequence-level splits. For small vehicles, sample or crop the training imagery without leaking frames from the same source into test. Keep the DAWN and nighttime clips as evaluation data. [Sakaridis et al.](https://people.ee.ethz.ch/~csakarid/SFSU_synthetic/)
- Temporal aggregation such as TransVOD can make isolated detections more consistent, at the cost of additional temporal state and inference. Re-identification helps most when vehicles have enough pixels to distinguish them. Start with a matched-box association comparison and a camera-motion audit to justify either cost. [TransVOD](https://github.com/SJTU-LuHe/TransVOD)
- Stop and wrong-way rules use interpretable geometry. Red-light scoring uses a finite stop line, an interpolated crossing, and externally supplied intervals. Signal recognition alone still needs an association between a lamp and its controlled lane. The Bosch dataset is a signal detector reference, with distinct camera geometry from these CCTV clips. [Bosch Small Traffic Lights](https://github.com/bosch-ros-pkg/bstld)
- Ranking and operating-point tuning answer different questions. A model score becomes a calibrated probability only after checking reliability on held-out examples. For this system the meaningful calibrations are at the box IoU threshold and at event confirmation. Use a reliability plot and an independent validation set before fitting a confidence mapping. [Temperature scaling reference](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/guo17a.html)

### Using tracking to guide detection

| Method | How it uses previous frames | Fit for this project |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ByteTrack | Its second association pass links lower-score boxes to current tracks. The candidate boxes still come from the frame's original detector pass. [Paper and implementation](https://github.com/FoundationVision/ByteTrack) | Already serves as a baseline. Detection can contribute new evidence if the tracker asks the model to look in a smaller region. |
| CaTDet | A lightweight proposal network and a tracker choose regions for a stronger refinement network. The paper reports 5.1–8.7× fewer operations at the same mAP on its KITTI setup. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.00434) | The proposal-and-refinement structure fits the failure case. Our prototype reuses the same detector for both passes, so it tests recognition at an added inference cost. |
| SegTrackDetect | Its inference loop collects detections from chosen windows, feeds them back to the ROI predictor, and uses a modified SORT implementation to predict windows. [Framework](https://github.com/deepdrivepl/SegTrackDetect) · [Inference code](https://github.com/deepdrivepl/SegTrackDetect/blob/main/inference.py) | Closest public implementation. Reuse its experimental idea and keep the existing RT-DETR adapter to isolate the effect of changing where detection runs. |
| Tracktor | Its Faster R-CNN box head refines the previous track box using current image features. The implementation can align the frames first. [Paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_ICCV_2019/html/Bergmann_Tracking_Without_Bells_and_Whistles_ICCV_2019_paper.html) · [Tracker code](https://github.com/phil-bergmann/tracking_wo_bnw/blob/master/src/tracktor/tracker.py) | Suitable with a detector that accepts existing boxes as proposals. RT-DETR exposes object queries through a different interface. The reference repository is GPL-3.0. |
| CenterTrack and FGFA | CenterTrack's model takes the previous frame and a heatmap of tracks as inputs. FGFA aligns and combines features from nearby frames. [CenterTrack detector code](https://github.com/xingyizhou/CenterTrack/blob/master/src/lib/detector.py) · [FGFA paper and code](https://github.com/msracver/Flow-Guided-Feature-Aggregation) | Both combine temporal evidence within recognition. CenterTrack changes the model and training setup. The published FGFA implementation uses MXNet. Test model changes after checking whether the current detector can recover the target with a bounded local search. |

The first comparison uses the location of a prior-frame proposal as the search center. This is a short-horizon approximation to track prediction. A same-frame crop control separates some of the gain from simply magnifying the search region. Only a box supported by pixels in the current frame can feed the event rules. [Experiment and results](road_validation/TRACK_GUIDANCE.md)

## Datasets and access

| Dataset | What it can verify | What was available |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Foggy Driving | Real fog image detection and object size | Pinned archive available. Its 68 coarse images were used for the previous cutoff calibration. The 33 fine images are development data. The authors reserve the dataset for evaluation. [Data](https://people.ee.ethz.ch/~csakarid/SFSU_synthetic/) |
| DAWN | Independent adverse-weather image corpus | Acquired V1 `Fog.zip` and verified its contents and SHA-256. This run consumes the fog JPEGs and XML annotations only. The repository contains no archive or photographs. [Record and terms](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/766ygrbt8y/1) |
| GRAM-RTM | Fixed-camera vehicle association at 25/30 FPS | Acquired the public development kit. Urban1 image sequence is on SharePoint and redirected to Microsoft sign-in. The kit contains sufficient XML ground truth and fixed detections for the two reported experiments. Obtain author access to the image sequences to measure the same trackers with our detector. [Dataset and scientific-use conditions](https://gram.web.uah.es/data/datasets/rtm/index.html) |
| UA-DETRAC | Multiple fixed sites, darkness, rain, occlusion | The original training archive URL redirected to the lab page with HTML rather than a ZIP. Obtain official archives or a provenance-preserving research copy including sequence IDs, images, track annotations and ignored regions. Derived image-only mirrors lose information needed for a camera-disjoint tracking test. [Original lab](https://www.albany.edu/cnse/research/computer-vision-machine-learning-lab) |
| AI City 2021 Track 4 | Stalls, crashes and background negatives | The data/evaluation page describes release and submission access. The direct fetch returned HTTP 403 in this environment. Use the organizer's current registration instructions and approved data link to obtain the videos and timestamps. [Challenge data](https://www.aicitychallenge.org/2021-data-and-evaluation/#:~:text=Track%204) |
| VisDrone | Small objects, crowding, video tracking | The public package has drone imagery. It is a useful small-object stress test, with camera motion and viewpoints unlike fixed CCTV. [Dataset](https://github.com/VisDrone/VisDrone-Dataset) |
| AVDL and Constellation | Environmental and perspective variation | AVDL releases arrays from virtual detection lines across 50 cameras, rather than full tracking video. Constellation has high-angle fixed intersection imagery and a published image detection benchmark. Neither supplied timestamped violations for this run. [AVDL](https://zenodo.org/records/6274296) · [Constellation](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11263-026-03029-1) |
| NJIT and Zenodo | Candidate alert behavior | Acquired the original demo footage and public source logs. Kept raw assets out of the repository. The legacy road samples are development clips. The two new nighttime views were used once under frozen settings. [NJIT demo](https://web.njit.edu/~cliu/NJDOT/DEMOS.html) · [Zenodo intersection footage](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.14041920) |

The next training input is Foggy Cityscapes-refined or licensed fixed-camera footage with complete box labels and source identities. For the former, register for Cityscapes, accept the dataset terms, and obtain the Foggy Cityscapes images and corresponding fine labels from the official download page. Keep the test images and all frames from held-out surveillance cameras outside that training set. For red and stopped events, obtain timestamped, identity-linked examples and longer negative recordings from new cameras. Those are the two main inputs missing for a defensible deployment claim. [Cityscapes download](https://www.cityscapes-dataset.com/downloads/)
